## Scene quality bar (set 18 Nov 2026 — every scene, in chat and on the page)
Every short scene idea is expanded into a complete, long, original, professional-level Telugu comedy scene. No ordinary, predictable or basic dialogue.
1. Powerful punch dialogues — fresh and unexpected; discard the first obvious joke, write the third or fourth idea.
2. Natural character comedy — each laugh comes from who is speaking (personality, slang, tags, reactions).
3. Non-stop comedy — no filler or plain information lines; every line is a setup, punch, counter or reaction.
4. Comedy twists — at least three: a funny misunderstanding, a counter that flips who is winning, and a late surprise/reversal.
5. Strong timing — setup → escalation → punch over 2–3 lines; punch word last; short reaction after big punches.
6. Character-to-character punches — tease, insult, counter, out-do; nobody wins twice in a row; include a counter-of-a-counter.
7. Trailer-level — at least 5 quotable highlight lines (listed as "Trailer highlights").
8. Explosive ending — the last line is the biggest, most unexpected laugh (reversal, callback or payoff of a plant); never a plain line or a moral.
9. Extra slang and expressions (updated 19 Nov 2026) — at least 5 extra slang words/expressions per scene, spread across characters, natural and in-voice; no word more than twice; never in place of the punch word.
Before delivering: a punch-up pass that cuts or rewrites any ordinary, predictable or explained line.

## Comedy craft upgrade (26 Nov 2026 — every scene, in chat and on the page)
- Quality over quantity. Natural flow: every line answers what the previous speaker actually said; real talk with reactions and pauses; no filler; no explained jokes.
- Punch comedy: real counters that turn the speaker's own words or boast against him; proper setups (plant → build → punch, punch word last); mostly short punches, sometimes a long build; never repeat a mechanism; don't force a punch into every line.
- Twists grow from the scene (confident claim turned around, small misunderstanding snowballs, escape makes it worse, one detail exposes the truth, last reply reverses the "winner"); plant clues early.
- Distinct voices and kinds of punch per character; replacement characters get fresh, stronger lines in their own style, not renamed lines.
- Flexible structure: opening → setup → development → punches → natural turn → payoff ending (callback). Use library mechanisms, never lines; invent new techniques; no formula twice.
- Quality check before showing (opening, flow, voices, counters, punches, twists, pacing, no filler, ending, originality); rewrite anything weak. The web app runs a separate Polish pass (on by default) that scores the draft and rewrites weak lines, shown under "Quality check".
