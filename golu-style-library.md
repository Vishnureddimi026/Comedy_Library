# Golu — Character & Dialogue Style Library (short project copy)

Full doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/KGQLDEiDNpnBnYj7rJBage
Golu is an original character (not from the films; first named Babloo, renamed Golu on 24 Nov 2026), DJ Vicky's jodi. Examples are all newly written.

## Core
- Vicky's childhood friend from the same Hyderabad gully; drives the DJ van, carries speakers, fixes wires (his whole "technical team").
- Engine: panic outside, loyalty inside. Panics at everything; Vicky panics at nothing.
- Traits: money tally of everything Vicky owes; quits every scene, never leaves; loyal blurter (tells the truth or backs the lie with wrong details); superstitious vs Vicky's fake science; grumbles but does the job.

## Dialogue patterns
1. Rising alarm ending in "విక్కీఈ…!"  2. The tally (numbers in every complaint)  3. Quit then stay ("పక్కా లాస్ట్")  4. Wrong backup  5. Question pile  6. Superstition rule ("మా దాదీ చెప్పింది")  7. Soft turn (one quiet loyal line).

## Slang (Hyderabadi Dakhni-mix)
హౌ, నక్కో, కైకూ, కిత్తా, క్యా బోల్తే, బోలే తో, ఖాలీపీలీ, ఏక్‌దమ్, పూరా, పక్కా, హల్లు హల్లు, చుప్ రే, బైగన్. Vicky = విక్కీ భాయ్ / విక్కీఈ…! / మామూ (money). సార్ for police/teachers, అన్నా for dons, అమ్మా for Vicky's mother.
Never: Vicky's English tags (వన్ మినిట్, ఓకే?, బాయ్), Uttarandhra words, mocking any community.

## Timing with Vicky
Beat: Golu long rising panic → Vicky one cool line → Golu bigger panic with a number → Golu gives in. Patterns: interrupted tally, echo panic, delayed blurt, quit at the peak, role-swap ending (Golu calm, Vicky panics).
Scene shape: Golu alarm → Vicky plan → Golu's wrong backup worsens it → twist only Golu's worry prepared for → Vicky takes credit.

## Rules
Numbers in every argument; practical worries; his worry is right once per scene; insults Vicky but never betrays him; not stupid (only he knows the equipment); never actually leaves.
Check: louder/longer than Vicky? number or practical worry? Dakhni not English? sets up or lands a punch? only Golu could say it?

## Pair engines (Cool vs Panic)
Alarm vs vibe · money tally vs lifestyle bluff · quit-and-return · loyal blurter vs topic change · superstition vs fake science · childhood friends trading insults, same side at the end.
